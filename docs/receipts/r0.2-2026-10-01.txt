4ca8f08 at the bench's fuel 10^8 does not finish the 8-entity prism:
stack overflow after 305 s at 2.1 GB (its walks scale with the fuel
cap); its prism rows therefore use fuel 1000, which the differential
oracle shows is sufficient. Growth: 4ca8f08 ≈ n^2.3 (prism); R0.2
5× entities → 6.1× time (prism 200 → 1,000), 4× → 6.0× (plate
1,604 → 6,404). The excess over n log n is construction, which pushes
one entity at a time onto list stores (O(n) each); the checks
themselves are sorts and sweeps.

receipt (arr-plate 40,004 segments 5.31 s, sol-rects 24,000
