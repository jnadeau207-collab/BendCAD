## 6. Audit trail (no semantic bugs in close-out)

No production-code semantic bugs were found during this session's close-out:
every rerun passed on the first fresh-binary attempt with zero source
changes. Two kernel-boundary findings from implementation are carried
honestly in `docs/c16.3-laws.md`, not worked around:

1. The TypeScript evaluator proves closed-solid at every admitted size, but
   the BendTT verdict kernel reports a TS/kernel mismatch once endpoint
   forcing (production div/mod index arithmetic in `edge_a`/`edge_b`)
   passes roughly 48 edges. F2 is therefore proven small (N in {2,3} at
   ring 8, M in {8,16} at 2 sections); production sizes are carried by
   bit-exact pins at NPTS 17/33/65 and the E2E oracle sweep with C04
   instance certification of every admitted size.
2. The verdict kernel rejects all concrete F64 arithmetic in laws, even in
   uninspected positions (probed). Laws run over law-local dummy
   coordinates; production coordinates are covered by bit-exact pins.

empty-file sha (they raced a rebuild); this session rebuilt via
