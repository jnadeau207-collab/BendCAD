## Outcome

- **Refusal g6-tsfc-not-closed**: target TSFC <= 1.05 lb/lbf/h at 1500 N; computed 1.193 lb/lbf/h with the best measured RDC pressure gain (-6.84%) and the 5% guide-vane loss; the Brayton baseline gives 1.102; physics: the deck closes 1.05 only with a net RDC equivalent-available-pressure gain of 10.5% at TIT 1150 K; every measured RDC pressure gain found is negative
- **Refusal g6-rdc-cell-size-not-fit**: target annular RDC whose channel holds its detonation cells inside the 272 mm envelope; computed one wave in a 228.0 mm annulus needs 290.6 m/s fill (Mach 0.592) through a 4.98 mm channel, 0.53 cell widths; physics: with cells near 9.38 mm at the delivery pressure the fill height of 12 cells cannot be met at a subsonic fill velocity in a channel at least one cell wide; it fits once the cell is at most 4.83 mm, which needs more hydrogen or oxygen enrichment than the brief's 10% hydrogen energy
- **Refusal g6-turbine-tip-clearance**: target cold turbine tip clearance <= 0.15 mm that stays positive at temperature (brief section 4.4); computed cold 0.15 mm closes 0.520 mm at 43842 r/min: running -0.370 mm (rub); a positive running gap needs 0.57 mm cold; physics: the Waspaloy rotor grows 0.812 mm thermally and 0.074 mm by spin, while the monolithic SiC/SiC shroud (6.0e-6/K) grows only 0.366 mm; the brief fixes the shroud as part of the CMC monolith, so no carrier ring can match the rotor
