  pairs report estimated with unresolved boxes (journeys
  `section.box_estimated/mapped`, ~150 s each). An exact
  interval line-plus-clip path would certify them; not built.

  subdivides to fuel before admitting defeat (~150 s worst
  case); a degeneracy pre-check would fail fast. Slanted
  planes and grazing planes are not journeyed.

  costs ~15 s on a 12-face solid (`interf.aabb_trap` 14.6 s,
  no journey bound). Parallel search with early exit would
  cut it; not built.
