## 6. Gate refusals and deferrals

1. **STL of four parts (REFUSED).** BendCAD's general tessellator meshes
   planar faces, cylinders bounded by one or two rings, and Bezier
   patches. It does not mesh faces trimmed on curved surfaces (a cylinder
   with a cut-out, torus, cone or sphere faces after a boolean). In this
   run it wrote open meshes for those parts (measured: impeller 24,277
   unpaired edges, casing 17,660, cone 840, motor 2,256 and 68 % of its
   volume missing; `calc/stl_check.json`). The kernel now checks every
   general-path mesh for closure and refuses with
   `tess-mesh-not-closed` instead of publishing it (E2E
   `tess.curved_trim_refused`). Fix: a parameter-space mesher for
   trimmed curved faces. Workaround for printing: the STEP files.
2. **Certified clearance (CLOSED 2026-11-03).** The owner reversed the
   deferral. Coaxial meridian footprints, a planar projection bound and a
   per-face prefilter let the branch-and-bound close all three impeller
   pairs in 79-143 s each (receipt `docs/receipts/clearance-2026-11-03`).
   Sampling had put the cone gap at 1.80 mm; the certified minimum is the
   design's 1.50 mm.
3. **Impeller natural frequencies (REFUSED).** The impeller FE at element
   size 8 mm (coarser than the 4 mm blades) ran 11 h 40 min wall /
   68 CPU-hours without finishing; a blade-resolving mesh (H <= 2.4 mm)
   is out of reach until the speed work. The COMPUTED blade estimate
   above covers the blade mode only.
4. **Minimum wall and machinability (REFUSED, time).** The casing wall
   certified [0, 3.0] mm: sound, but its lower bound is not useful. The
   impeller wall and the 5-axis reachability check (tool d 10, flute 30,
   holder d 25 x 60) had not finished after 8 h. Design intent: 3 mm
   sheet, 4 mm blades, 5 mm root fillets >= tool radius 5 mm.

## 8. Kernel defects found and fixed during this run

- STL export was effectively quadratic: chord tolerance was used as a
  maximum segment length (a 454 mm ring got 5,705 segments instead of
  126), and the output string join copied the whole file per append.
  Large-cylinder export went from > 900 s to 0.4 s.
- Tessellation under-reported angle and chord: circles were sampled
  uniformly in parameter (max step 27 % over the reported value). Now
  sampled uniformly in angle; the c10 suite checks exact polygon volumes.
- Hole patterns with odd per-circle counts left caps untriangulated;
  revolve rings collapsed one vertex to the origin. Fixed; covered by
  c06/c10 volume checks.
- General-path meshes are now checked for closure and refused instead of
  published open.
- Booleans: a K1-first path silently returned the outer solid unchanged
  when the tool lay entirely inside it (no cavity). K1 now declines
  crossing-free operands there and the classifier handles them.
- Certified clearance: witness upper bounds, coaxial and meridian bounds,
  face-level radial floors and a quantised, size-tiebroken search order;
  coaxial cases certify in < 1 s (they did not finish before).

## 9. Timing

This gate is not usable at its current speed: evaluating the blower takes
about 1 h (the 10-blade impeller dominates), every command re-evaluates
the whole design, and individual analyses ran from minutes to > 8 h.
The owner's ruling (2026-10-31) is a speed packet before G5 with a target
near 1000x for assembly work and no loss of accuracy, provenance or
robustness.
