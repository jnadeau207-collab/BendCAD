# C05.1 receipt — 2026-09-29 (no spurious solutions)

Packet: C05.1 closes a class of solver bugs where a residual is zero
on geometry the constraint does not mean (contract §3.4).

- `K_angle` is directed: its residual is zero only at θ. Before, the
  residual was the sine of the error, which θ+180° also satisfied.
- Horizontal and vertical are angular and scale-invariant, so a
  collapsed line no longer satisfies them. h+v on one line is now a
  certified conflict naming exactly those two, matching legacy
  `MutuallyExclusiveAxes`.
- Solved requires admissible geometry (`geo_ok`): every line a
  constraint relies on must be longer than `tol.lin`, and every radius
  must exceed it. This is the legacy `SelfEndpointCollapse` intent.
- Radii must be positive at the start and on every accepted step
  (`rad_ok`).
- Laws pin "ok iff solved", the geometry veto and the radius domain in
  general. Closed laws discriminate each trap: every one fails under
  the previous solver.

Predecessor: 6a4a500 (C05; its receipt, c05-2026-09-29.txt, remains
the record of that commit). Contract: docs/c05-contract.md
(§3.4, §9, §10, §11 updated).
