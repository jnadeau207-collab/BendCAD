## 5. Semantic bugs

No production-code semantic bug was found in this packet. The audit
trail, stated honestly:

- Two first-run E2E misses were suite-side predicate bugs: a
  `ninst` substring match and an n2/n3 creation-node filter in the
  journey predicates. Both fixed in the suite; the kernel output
  was verified correct in each case. No production line changed.
- Harness (not kernel): the c06 MCP tool-set expectation gained
  the intentional `assembly` entry (§4); the c08
  chained.mixed_refused single miss was an environmental flake
  (passes on rerun; the exact apply reproduces the expected
  unsupported-op/mixed-solid-relations refusal on both the bc19cd67
  and e8d1e6f2 binaries).
- Law-harness notes (not kernel): the exact kind-4→`rows_axis`
  identity law was replaced by the arity law `mate_rows_axis_len`
  (axis is the unique 2-row builder; exact unfolding took 91 s in
  F64 soft-float terms; exact identity left to pins/E2E per
  D-axis-law). Two first-choice mutants were replaced after
  failing-check blowup on giant stuck F64 terms (arm-1→rows_coinc
  spun 38+ min, solve_rep solved! burned 42 CPU-min); the
  replacements (arm-1→`[]` killed via `mate_rows_fix`, lin_go
  `=n`→`=N` killed via `lin_cons`) kill in ~25–40 s each.

Touch note (honest estimate, not a bug): face-touching boxes report
`relation uncertain status=estimated` through C11 subdivision (6.6 s)
instead of a certified disjoint the kernel cannot establish; the
journey accepts certified-disjoint or estimated-uncertain and forbids
any interference claim. That is the designed failure semantic.
