`p2(n)` is 2^-n. `col_max` is the largest squared column norm.
λ₀ is `p2(10) · max(col_max, 1)`. The `1` floors that squared norm
before the scale: a Jacobian whose columns are all shorter than 1,
including a zero column, would otherwise make λ₀ zero and drop the
√λ row that keeps every column full rank. The clamp stays
[2^-30 λ₀, 2^60 λ₀]. Two rules changed the damping, not one.
Iteration starts at the floor 2^-30 λ₀, and `lm_step`'s accept arm
sets `done` when the updated λ is above the ceiling. The reject
arm already stopped on that ceiling. An earlier edit wrote the
ceiling as p2(30)·(new λ) and put it below the start, so the first
rejected step stopped the solve. That edit is not this tree.

on the CPU, because BANGS is 0. pow2!(20) with an 800-step leaf,
compiled by the side scheduler, 16 threads, `--gpu off`: prints
2081423360 in 0.01 s. The same binary `--gpu on` exits 1:

  needs concurrent managed access, which WSL2's lack)

sentence. No GPU timing exists, and none is claimed.
