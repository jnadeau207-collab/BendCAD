## 5. THE CONE FINDING (load-bearing, needs owner review)

Claim: no honest G.GV_co cone through the single-edge blend's exact
tangent-point vertices can satisfy q_on_r (hence C04 brep_checked and
C07 check), so intent §2's "quarter-cone when they differ" + L9 + the
§5 cone journey are unsatisfiable. The kernel's refusal is correct
per §0/L1–L3 (uncertainty is failure; never publish a stand-in).

Evidence (all measured on the pinned toolchain with compiled probes;
reproducible from src/c03/ops.bend + src/c09/feat.bend):

- The 4 tangent vertices sit at exact 0.0 in the surface-normal
  directions (box at origin forces y=0/z=0), so q_on_r's 1-ulp corner
  neighborhoods extend ONLY along surface-tangent directions.
- At p0=(1,0,0) (R0=1,R1=2,H=10): cone residual +4.835638167094562e-14
  (exact sign, positive); the two x-corner implicits differ by exactly
  3.37052637048715e-31. A corner flip needs |I| < ~1.7e-31, but one k
  ulp moves I by ~2.8e-16: hitting the window needs ~1e15 samples
  (~60 years at 2 µs each). The only pass is exact Sz (I == 0).
- Sz needs (1+f_sq(k))·A² = N·m2 exactly. Scans of ±8192 k-ulps
  around the naive k0 (root provably inside at ≈-170 ulps) find no
  Sz and no flip at any single point, let alone all four; per-point
  bands are empty too. With the exact axis the equation needs
  k² = 1/101, impossible for a double (101 prime); with any rounded
  axis each candidate needs an independent ~2^-60 algebraic miracle.
- Tilting the cone cannot help: residual and corner span both scale
  with the tilt, keeping their ~1e16 ratio; only a ~60° kink (a
  different, dishonest solid) would open the window.
- Contrast the cylinder case (R0=R1): axis-exact + integer tangent
  math gives Sz exactly — certifiable by luck of alignment. The cone
  has no such alignment for any R0 != R1.

Consequence: (fillet BASE (edge N) R0 R1) with R0 != R1 is refused as
numerical-uncertainty | why: surface-not-representable | fix: report
this design. The e2e cone journey cannot pass as written. Requested
owner amendment (MASTER_PLAN §11): either name a cone refusal
(e.g. fillet-cone-not-certified, invalid-input or unsupported-op) and
repoint §5/L9/e2e at it, or narrow the admitted variable-radius blend
to a certifiable family. Until then S6 blocks the C09 closeout flip.
