## 0. Laws gaps found and fixed

Review of the draft law file against the implementation found:

1. `trim_quad` advertised but missing: the ops.bend header and the
   surfx conventions both name "the C06 quad-trim check" as part of
   the fail-closed path, but no such function existed. Implemented
   `O.trim_quad` + 17 laws (§R).
2. `Measure` beyond the type: contract §7 listed measure computation
   as pending with no constructor/projector coverage at all.
   Implemented `O.ms_box` (+ `Y.ms_*` projectors) with 15 laws (§S);
   contract §7/§8 updated.
3. Result-protocol holes: `intent_fm`/`intent_shells` projectors,
   `matfx_tag`, `sok_*` round-trips and wrong-arm defaults for the
   iv/s/fx/body/part/ms arms, and kind preservation through `Y.serr`
   had no laws. `surf_kind_tag`/`surf_kind_show` had no laws. Added
   32 laws (§P).
4. Diagnosis paths unpinned at runtime: `att_tag` had no direct law,
   `diag_brep` had no fuel-exhaustion law and no diag-text pins, and
   the test suites exercised zero staged-diagnosis paths. Added 5
   laws (§Q) and tests/c06/diag.bend (29 runtime pins).
5. Adversarial box pins lived only in prose: the FP-adversarial
   coordinates, negative corner, and fin/pos gate ordering had laws
   but no runtime tests. Added 4 pins to tests/c06/box.bend.

Deliberately NOT built (contract §7, fail-closed, pinned by
neg_evop_* laws): cyl/sph/cone/tor builders, general
extrude/revolve, hole/pocket/pad surgery, xform, forest compounds
and merge restamping, per-node partial cache reuse. Partial reuse is
observationally transparent through EvRes, so it needs no law until
rebuild counts become observable. The Spec/join_specs bulk-builder
machinery in ops.bend has no consumer yet; it is the scaffold for
forest compounds, not dead weight to delete.
