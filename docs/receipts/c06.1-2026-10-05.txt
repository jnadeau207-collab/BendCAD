laws/c06_build.bend, one process per law, five at a time, all from the
same snapshot: 24 of 25 ALL PROOFS CHECK, taking 2 s to 6137 s each
(ev_bore is the longest). The 25th, ear_holes16 (a 100 x 100 square with
16 holes, 98 triangles), ran 4 h 07 min at flat memory without finishing
and was stopped. It was replaced by ear_holes4, which pins the same
triangle count formula (vertices + 2·holes − 2 = 26) and passed:
ALL PROOFS CHECK in 3214 s, run alone on the final file. Ear clipping at
scale is covered by E2E (100 and 400 holes, watertight, genus checked),
and the 1,600-hole case was measured in a475bd7.

Discrimination: each new zone, trigonometry and placement law was run
against a mutant of the code it pins, and every mutant failed. The
mutants: interval env uses hi for lo; torus without R − r; positivity
on hi; or-for-and zone order; prism certified; sine series truncated
to 2 terms; exact quarter path removed; `pl_exact` constant true;
`padd`/`pmul` replaced by `iadd`/`imul`. `zone_unordered` was
tightened after its first form did not catch the or-for-and mutant.

Profile and evaluator laws were stuck before this packet: `F64.read` and
`F64.show` are bodiless `law`s, so the checker never reduces them. The
decimal reader in sx.bend is checker-evaluable and bit-identical to
`F64.read` on 23 probe strings, including fallbacks. It differs only on
`inf` and `+5`, which are now names.
