restatements (`x_go(False{}, ...) == GR_no{...}` where `x_go` is
defined as exactly that arm): 10 in c16.1 F3, 9 in c16.2 F3, 8 in
c16.3 F4. All 27 are deleted. In their place, 19 laws drive a
representative out-of-domain value through the production entry the

`BA.blade_rep`, `C.flat_sc`) and pin the exact refusal record —
code, fix, what, node rendering and FailKind — quantifying every
input the refusal does not depend on. Eight refusal codes carry no
law because their gate chains need F64 arithmetic the verdict kernel
cannot compute (`rt`, `size`, `lo`, `sp`, `tc`, `ts`, plus the sheet
grammar paths): pins and neg journeys are their cover, recorded per
code in `docs/c16.N-laws.md`. No `src/` change was needed — no law
exposed a defect — so no rebuild was run; pins, journeys and G3 are
unaffected and stand as receipted above.
