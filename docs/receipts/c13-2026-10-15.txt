### 4.1 c05: 34 laws, 33 rows, every family covered

c05 never had a planted mutant. It has 33 rows now: sign/sgn algebra
flips in `src/base/par.bend` (2) and `src/base/ex.bend` (5:
negation, uncertain-dominance, add/mul taint), arrange gate flips in
`src/c05/arrange.bend` (3), and solver flips in `src/c05/solve.bend`
(21: run/lm gates, conflict/classify branches, show strings, gather
length, coincidence/pair/line/par/perp/horiz/dist validity, solved
and radii positivity). Two laws are inductions over the compiler
prelude with no implementation to break (`nat_eq_refl` over
`Nat.is_eq`, `or_true_r` over `Bool.or`); their mutants falsify the
stated property (`is_eq(x,1n+x)`, `or(x,False)`) and the proofs fail,
which shows the proofs establish the claim rather than elaborate
vacuously. Both are labeled `is not vacuous` in the report.

The 34th law, `geometry_veto`, carries no row, by measurement rather
than by omission. Its proof is a one-step corollary application of
law 30 (`ok_iff_solved`), so every genuine falsifier must break
`classify_go`'s False arm — and every such falsifier sends the
checker into failure-driven unfolding search through the F64
stationarity term instead of a verdict. Measured three ways, all on
the pinned toolchain under `bendcap`: an `ok_of` False arm (13+ min,
51 CPU-min, ~10 GB, killed without output), a concrete `Sv_ok` False
arm (11 min, 34 CPU-min, ~10 GB, killed without output), and a
falsified statement (`== True{}`, 30:00 timeout, 8666 CPU-s, 9.99 GB
max RSS, zero output; `/tmp/bendcad-mut-probe.log`). The passing
check is 143 s and every other c05 kill lands in seconds to ~5 min.
The family is covered — laws 29/30/31/32 kill fast — and the law
itself is proven in §3; what it cannot carry is a practical kill,
and the report does not pretend otherwise.
