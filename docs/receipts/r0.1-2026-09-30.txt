## 4. Changes to laws and pins (none weakened)

Restated because their subject changed, same meaning:
- conflict_names_violations: `Sv_conflict{dedup(viol(ds))}` (viol now
  lists every violated row's id; dedup keeps each once, in order).
- classify_is_solved_at: over rows recomputed by `rows_at`
  (classify recomputes rows at the final point, as before).
- steps_keep_radii_positive: the acceptance gate is `rows_at`'s ok,
  still exactly `rad_ok ∧ rows_ok` at the trial point.
- solved_needs_geometry: `geo_ok` takes the parameter tree `ptb(ps)`.
- ok_iff_solved (helper def): the stationarity test uses `jtr_nrm`.
- dense_len → gather_len: the step vector has one entry per parameter.
Removed with its subject: zeros_len.

New closed laws (each checked to discriminate):
- ok_solve_zero_pivot — False on the build without the zero-dropping
  fix (exact zeros reaching a pivot gave NaN and hid redundancy 27).
- ok_solve_clusters_merge — redundancy across clusters in global order.
- neg_solve_conflict_localized — a consistent cluster is never blamed.

Re-pinned (Givens vs Gram–Schmidt rounding; dof/red unchanged):
- ok_solve_linear: 6.004550604512308e-30 → 6.004550601573572e-30.
- ok_solve_redundant_named: 3.939192985791677 → 3.939192985791676.
- pos suite: 11 `pos-sol-*` strings, each asserted to keep its
