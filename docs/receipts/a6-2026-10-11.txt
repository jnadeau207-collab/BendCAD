## 1. What was wrong (measured)

Bend's guide: a law states a fact for every input (`for x: T`), is
proven by a def of the same name through case analysis, induction and
`%` rewriting, and `--verdict` rechecks it with the Lean-proven BendTT
kernel; the bend2 checker alone "has no proof and may have bugs". Its
README: "F32 is axiomatic: nothing about floating point can be proven."

BendCAD at da48c7a (before A6): 1,934 laws, 164 with any quantifier;
about 1,770 were `{f(fixed input) == fixed output}` closed by `{==}`,
which is a unit test run inside the checker's normalizer (c08_build
laws ran for hours; 34 of 40 never got a verdict). No law file passed
`--verdict`. Probes on the pin:

- ground Nat, U32, U64 and List laws pass `--check-only` and `--verdict`;
- a ground F64 law (`1.0 + 2.0 == 3.0`) passes `--check-only` and fails
  `--verdict`: the kernel runs out of fuel evaluating the fork's
  soft-float (`bendtt f64.bendtt` → "In f64_add: out of fuel"; a 1000×
  fuel kernel overflows its stack);
- F64 values that flow through a law symbolically, and F64 code in an
  untaken branch, pass `--verdict`;
- `laws/c00.bend` and `laws/c01.bend` fail `--verdict`; every src
  package reaches F64.
