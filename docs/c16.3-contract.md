# C16.3 blade sections — contract (normative)

C16.3 builds compressor/fan blades from NACA 4-digit sections and honest
section reports. `(naca4 M P T C NPTS)` is the section, `(blade S0 S1 ...
Z TWIST TAPER)` the ruled-loft stack, `blade NODE` the reporter.

## 1. Positive list

- `(naca4 M P T C NPTS)`: max camber M percent, camber position P tenths,
  max thickness T percent, chord C (any finite positive expression),
  NPTS uniform stations per surface. Admitted: M 0-9, P 0-9 with P
  nonzero when M is nonzero, T 06-24, NPTS in {17, 33, 65}. Sections are
  perpendicular-offset NACA 4-digit rings (half-thickness with the 0.1036
  closed-T E coefficient, camber line, normal from sqrt(1+dyc^2)); the
  trailing edge closes chordally from the last lower station to the
  single trailing-edge vertex. The node publishes the unit-span slab so
  a section measures, tessellates and exports like any solid.
- `(blade S0 S1 ... Z TWIST TAPER)`: 2 to 8 naca4 sections stacked over
  span Z, linear twist schedule to TWIST degrees in {0, 10, 20, 30} about
  the quarter-chord stacking axis, linear taper to TAPER tip-chord
  percent in {50, 75, 100}. Sections must share NPTS and chord; digits
  may vary per station (root camber to tip symmetry is a ruled stack).
  Twist uses the certified sin/cos interval evaluator; taper leans the
  stack about the leading edge, so the stacking-axis deviation is
  0.25*C*(1-TAPER/100) on symmetric sections. Blades are closed solids
  (root cap, tip cap, bilinear-ruled walls over the section polygons).
- `blade NODE`: one row per section (digits, chord, stacking height,
  certified area, centroid, max-thickness and max-camber intervals), the
  certified stacking-axis deviation and the twist schedule. All intervals
  are interval-arithmetic containments of the stored brep's values.
- Line edges are elevated beziers through exact end corners (the
  build.bend general-line convention: `pa+(pb-pa)` rounding cannot fail
  the embedding check). Walls are bilinear beziers through exact
  corners; caps are planes at the exact stored stations.

## 2. Refusals (first-wins order per verb)

naca4: `naca-digits-not-admitted` (unsupported-op), then
`naca-points-not-admitted` (unsupported-op), then `degenerate-feature`
(invalid-input, chord not finite positive). Malformed forms are
`unknown-op`.

blade: `blade-sections-not-admitted` (unsupported-op, not 2-8
sections), `twist-not-admitted` (unsupported-op, shared C09 string),
`taper-not-admitted` (unsupported-op), `degenerate-feature`
(invalid-input, span not finite positive),
`blade-stack-not-ruled` (uncertain: a section is not an naca4 node,
is missing, or the stack mixes points or chord), then per-section
`naca-digits-not-admitted` / `naca-points-not-admitted` /
`degenerate-feature` re-validation, then the ruled uniformity check
(`blade-stack-not-ruled`). Malformed forms are `unknown-op`.

`blade` on a non-blade node (including naca4 sections) is
`blade-needs-blade` (invalid-input); on a missing node `unknown-node`;
on a failed node `nothing bladed`. Zones, pockets, holes and patterns
on blades answer the existing refusals
(`topology-not-certified-over-zone`,
`features-need-an-extruded-body`).

## 3. Mass, intent, lineage

- naca4 slabs and blades store the certified prismoidal volume interval:
  exact for bilinear-ruled walls by Simpson on the quadratic area
  schedule (mid-ring area from the averaged ring). Mass volume is the
  interval, not the point widening. Area keeps the loose 24·bm^2 bound.
- Intent materials: both verbs are baseless (`create`); sections are
  parametric specs, not geometric bases, and blade re-validates every
  section from its AST. Counts stay `any`.
- Content-key tags are 39 naca4, 40 blade; the blade key prefixes the
  section count before the section ids, then twist, taper and span.

## 4. Boundary names and durability

- Faces carry durable indices: walls 0..(N-1)*M-1 in (section, ring)
  order, root cap (N-1)*M, tip cap (N-1)*M+1, where M = 2*NPTS-2.
  Names derive from canonical construction order, so parameter edits
  keep every name stable. `show`/`apply` replay is byte-exact.

## 5. STEP, archive, tessellation

- Blades export as ordinary closed solids: 2 plane caps and (N-1)*M
  bilinear-bezier walls; one E2E asserts the exact face-kind split on
  read-back. No builder emits cone or torus surfaces, so the
  section-3 kernel limit on CONICAL_SURFACE is untriggered.
- Archive round-trips blades brep-preserving. Tessellation triangulates
  planar caps by boundary ring and evaluates twisted walls on the fixed
  8x8 interior grid through the shared bicubic evaluator; the grid is a
  documented resolution, not an error-certified approximation.

## 6. Complexity and budgets

- Builders are linear in output size (vertices N*M, edges (2N-1)*M,
  faces (N-1)*M+2, M = 2*NPTS-2). Section lookup inside flat builders
  is bounded by the 8-section admission (constant). An 8-section
  NPTS-65 blade (1024 vertices, 1920 edges, 898 faces) builds under
  30 s; `blade` reports in milliseconds per section. No builder changes
  the complexity of any shared evaluator path.

## 7. Manifold-solid profile (normative stage list)

A blade or slab is published only if `brep_checked` with
`P_manifold_solid` returns Tok. Stages, in order: incidence; edge-use
balanced (every edge once each way); loop-closure-or-trim; face-kind;
ownership; embedding; vertex-link; shell-connectivity; coincident
vertices; segment-crossing; orientation. Any failing stage refuses
publication with the uncertain verdict naming the stage.

## 8. Deferrals (explicit, carried, not silent)

- D-blade-checkbez: blades are certified at build time (all eleven
  manifold stages including embedding), but `check` reports
  `face-check-unsupported` on every bilinear-bezier wall — the same
  verdict C09 tapered lofts and C16.2 tapered ducts carry, since
  face-check does not cover bezier faces. Non-prismatic stacks
  (twisted, tapered or mixed-digit) additionally report one
  `orientation-uncertain` line: the sampling ray-parity audit hits the
  sub-resolution trailing-edge wedge degenerately and honestly declines
  to decide. Prismatic stacks report the wall lines only.
- D-blade-curve: section properties are certified intervals of the
  stored NPTS-gon, not of the smooth NACA curve; the chordal gap to the
  smooth curve is a resolution note, not a certified bound. tmax/cmax
  are maxima over admitted stations.
- D-blade-feat: features (pads, pockets, holes) refuse on blades with
  `features-need-an-extruded-body`; root cooling holes are future work
  requiring extruded-body feature support, with strictly-inside
  footprints when it lands.
- D-blade-lean: the stack leans only through taper about the leading
  edge; explicit lean/sweep parameters are not admitted.
- D-blade-cosine: stations are uniform (exact binary fractions); cosine
  clustering at the leading edge is not admitted.
