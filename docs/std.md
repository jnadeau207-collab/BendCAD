- `tap` (tap_drill): ISO 2306:1972 drills for tapping, class 6H, the drill size nearest D - P; source: D and P from ISO 261 coarse pitches held in src/k2/thr.bend
- `shcs` (shcs_dk, shcs_k): ISO 4762 hexagon socket head cap screw, dk max (knurled head) and k max; source: westfieldfasteners.co.uk/Standards/ScrewBolt-SHCap-M.html
- `cbore` (cbore_d, cbore_t): DIN 974-1:2008 counterbore diameter for ISO 4762 without washer; depth k + allowance; source: engineersbible.com/counterbore-socket-iso
- `cskh` (csk_dk, csk_k): ISO 10642 hexagon socket countersunk head screw, dk theoretical max and k max, 90 degree head; source: westfieldfasteners.co.uk/Standards/ScrewBolt-SHCsk-M.html
- `csk` (csk_d): DIN 74 form F / ISO 15065 90 degree countersink for ISO 10642; source: engineersbible.com countersunk-iso table, reproduced at xometry.com/resources/machining/what-are-countersink-holes
- `hex` (hex_af): ISO 4032 hexagon nut width across flats s (ISO 272); source: ISO 4032 s nominal
