# C16.3 laws

Floats are axiomatic in proofs: no law evaluates concrete F64
arithmetic (the Lean kernel cannot validate it). Topology laws run over
the production pure-Nat index functions with law-local dummy
coordinates; numeric behavior is evidenced by bit-exact pins and E2E
oracles.

## F1 — section closure (1 law)

- `naca_loop_closed`: for every admitted NPTS tag, the production ring
  topology closes over a dummy-coordinate profile (head at 0, linked,
  below length). The ring-step core is shared with the brep edge and
  wall builders.

## F2 — blade closure (2 laws)

- `blade_n_closed`: for N in {2, 3} at ring 8, the production
  edge/wall/cap topology is a closed solid (loops closed, uses
  balanced, ends below, V-E+F=2).
- `blade_m_closed`: for M in {8, 16} at 2 sections, the same.

## F3 — counts (4 laws)

- `ring_segs_len`, `walls_len`: induction on the Nat counter; the
  recursive topology builders emit exactly their lengths.
- `blade_rows_len`, `blade_rows_nil`: induction on the section list;
  the report emits one row per section.

## F4 — refusal closure (8 laws)

- `fin_go_false`, `dg_go_false`, `np_go_false`, `ns_go_false`,
  `tw_go_false`, `tp_go_false`, `rl_go_false`, `bl_go_false`: each gate
  maps False to its exact refusal record. Stated over the Bool
  argument, never unfolding a builder.

## Mutants (one per family)

- F1: the ring step skips its successor.
- F2: a wall-loop use flips direction.
- F3: the report drops every section row.
- F4: bad twists are accepted.

## Deviation notes

- The F2 instances are small by kernel necessity, not by choice:
  the TypeScript evaluator proves closed-solid at every admitted size
  (check-only greens (N, 32) for N in 2..8 and (2, M) for M in
  32/64/128), but the BendTT verdict kernel reports a TS/kernel
  mismatch once endpoint forcing (production div/mod index arithmetic
  in edge_a/edge_b) passes roughly 48 edges: 20- and 48-edge
  closed-solid instances verdict clean while 96-edge instances fail,
  with identical law shapes. Production sizes are carried instead by
  bit-exact pins at NPTS 17/33/65 and the E2E oracle sweep with C04
  instance certification of every admitted size.
- Closure at (N, M) jointly is proven at N in {2, 3} x M=8 and N=2 x
  M in {8, 16}; universal counts plus the pins and E2E sweep carry
  the remaining sizes.
- The verdict kernel rejects all concrete F64 arithmetic in laws, even
  in uninspected positions (probed); hence dummy coordinates, with
  production coordinates covered by bit-exact pins.
